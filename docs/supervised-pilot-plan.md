# Supervised pilot checklist

This protocol does not establish completed testing or provider availability. Keep participant identities and internal operational notes private.

- Obtain separate permission for assessment testing and optional photos. Prior technology-installation permission does not establish testing consent.
- Have a qualified reviewer assess question clarity, recommendations and the distinction between family home checks and clinical assessments.
- Observe participants starting, answering, reading results, saving/resuming and requesting help on their usual devices. Record active completion time, confusing questions and assistance required. Allow breaks and stopping.
- Rehearse account recovery, role isolation, failed saves, photo permissions, consent withdrawal and backup restoration.
- Agree queue ownership, review frequency, absence coverage and a realistic response expectation before accepting public requests.
- Test approvals do not establish provider capacity. Record actual outcomes before marking any launch gate complete. Keep payments disabled.
- Validate the queued 10–15-minute assessment target through observed sessions before advertising it as achieved.
