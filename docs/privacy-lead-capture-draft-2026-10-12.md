## Proposed retention schedule

- Saved home checks and saved-plan receipts: 12 months after the last account activity, unless the person asks for earlier deletion or renews their request to keep them.
- Help requests and follow-up records: 24 months after the request closes. Open requests need quarterly staff review rather than indefinite unattended retention.
- Accepted mail job bodies: cleared immediately after email-provider acceptance, as implemented. Proposed retention for delivery status and consent/audit records: 24 months after the related item closes.
- Anonymous funnel counts: 31 days, as implemented. Removal occurs when a new event arrives, not on a guaranteed daily schedule.
- Proposed deletion handling: respond within 30 days; remove eligible active records and private photos, and let backup copies expire within a documented maximum of 90 days. Verify vendor backup/log retention first. Retain only records needed for a documented legal or billing obligation and explain exceptions to the requester.

Retention enforcement and vendor backup/log periods remain to be verified. Do not apply the consumer-plan schedule to professional clinical records without a separate reviewed policy.

## HomeCheck notice draft

When you save a home check, we store the selected check and your plan in your signed-in account. This can include home details, your answers about daily activities and health, your first name, verified email, and any ZIP code or phone number you provide. We also record your separate choices about plan email, contact from MyIntel, and sharing results.

An email saying your plan is saved contains your first name and a sign-in link. It does not contain your assessment answers or health details. MyIntel staff can see that you saved a plan, your contact details and consent choices. Staff can see the saved results through the lead queue only if you choose to share them. Saving a plan or requesting an email does not give permission for sales contact. The optional contact and sharing choices start unchecked.

If you request help, we store your request type, contact preference, verified contact details, ZIP code, and your consent choices. Any home check or photos are included only when you choose to share them. A professional receives information only after you separately authorize sharing with that named professional. A request does not book an appointment.

Supabase provides sign-in and private photo storage. Turso stores application records. Vercel hosts HomeCheck. Resend sends account-related messages and staff notifications. Staff notifications contain contact and request details, not health answers. We use anonymous daily event counts to understand whether people finish a check, save a plan, or request help. Those counts contain no answers, names, email addresses or case identifiers. Saving in an account and sharing with a professional are separate choices.

Proposed retention for approval: saved checks and plans are kept for 12 months after your last account activity. Closed help requests and related consent and follow-up records are kept for 24 months. You can ask for deletion sooner by emailing austin@myintelhome.com or calling (720) 989-1123. We will explain any records that must be retained and how deletion affects shared copies. Do not publish these retention promises until the process and backup periods are verified.

### HomeCheck plans and requests for help

HomeCheck is MyIntel's self-guided home check. When you save a plan, we store your selected check, its results, your first name and verified email, any phone number or ZIP code you provide, and your consent choices. Results may contain home, routine, mobility, falls, eating or drinking information. Your saved plan remains behind account sign-in.

If you ask us to email your plan, the message contains a sign-in link, not your answers. This is separate from permission for MyIntel to contact you. Staff can see your saved-plan receipt and contact choices; the lead queue reveals result details only when you consent to sharing. Requests for help store the service requested, preferred contact method, contact information, and consent history. Sharing results or photos with a named professional requires separate permission. No appointment is automatically booked.

We use Supabase for authentication and private photo storage, Turso for application data, Vercel for hosting, and Resend for email delivery. These services process the information needed to provide their functions. Staff email notifications contain contact and request details rather than assessment answers. HomeCheck's funnel measurement uses anonymous daily counts without identifying people or storing their answers.

Proposed retention for approval: saved checks and plan receipts are kept for 12 months after the last account activity; closed help requests and associated consent and follow-up records are kept for 24 months. Ask for access, correction or deletion at austin@myintelhome.com or (720) 989-1123. We will verify the request, explain any necessary retention exception, and explain which copies shared with others we cannot directly delete. Do not publish the proposed periods until the deletion process and vendor backup/log limits are confirmed.
